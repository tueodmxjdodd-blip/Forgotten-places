Places of Mystery

The Catacombs of Paris

Beneath the streets of Paris lies a network of underground tunnels known as the Catacombs. Originally limestone quarries, they became a burial place in the late 18th century when the city's cemeteries became overcrowded. Today, the tunnels contain the remains of millions of people. Their dark passages and unusual history have made them one of the world's most mysterious attractions.

Location: Paris, France

Aradale Mental Hospital

Located in Ararat, Victoria, Australia, Aradale was opened as a psychiatric hospital in 1867. The large complex once contained dozens of buildings and operated for more than a century. The hospital closed in the 1990s, but its historic buildings remain. Today, the site is known for its history, ghost stories and paranormal tours.

Location: Ararat, Victoria, Australia

Inside the Great Pyramids

The Great Pyramids of Egypt were built thousands of years ago as tombs for pharaohs. Their enormous stone structures, narrow passages and hidden chambers have inspired countless mysteries. Some legends tell of curses connected to ancient tombs, adding to the fascination surrounding these remarkable monuments. Their real history is just as intriguing as the stories told about them.

Location: Giza, Egypt

Aboveground Cemeteries, Guatemala

In Livingston, Guatemala, visitors can find cemeteries with graves built above the ground. This design is used in some places where the water table makes traditional underground burials difficult. Rows of raised tombs create a striking and unusual landscape. Their appearance and quiet surroundings have given these cemeteries a mysterious atmosphere.

Location: Livingston, Guatemala

Dracula and Transylvania, Romania

Transylvania is a Romanian region famous for its medieval towns, mountain scenery and vampire legends. Bran Castle is often linked to Dracula, the fictional vampire created by Bram Stoker. Although the connection between the castle and the character is not proven, the area's folklore and historic architecture make it a popular destination for people interested in mysterious stories.

Location: Bran, Transylvania, Romania

Interesting Facts

  • The Paris Catacombs are located underground.
  • Aradale Hospital opened in the 19th century.
  • The Great Pyramids were built as royal tombs.
  • Aboveground graves can be found in coastal areas.
  • Transylvania is associated with the Dracula legend.
