Why Are Places Forgotten?

There are many reasons why places become abandoned. Economic problems, natural disasters, wars and changing ways of life can leave once-busy places empty. Over time, nature slowly takes over, creating unusual landscapes and preserving traces of the past.

  • Abandoned factories and hospitals
  • Lost villages and ancient settlements
  • Historic places surrounded by legends
  • Buildings slowly reclaimed by nature
