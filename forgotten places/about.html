Our Story

Forgotten Places is a website dedicated to exploring abandoned buildings, lost cities and mysterious locations around the world. These places may have been left behind, but their history still lives on.

The purpose of this website is to introduce visitors to unusual destinations, share their stories and show how nature and time can transform places once filled with life.

Why This Topic?

I chose this topic because abandoned places are fascinating. They combine history, architecture, mystery and forgotten memories. Every location has something unique to tell, making it interesting to discover how it looked in the past and what remains today.

What Can You Explore?

  • Lost Cities — places abandoned by their inhabitants.
  • Abandoned Buildings — forgotten structures and their histories.
  • Haunted Places — locations surrounded by legends and mysteries.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring attention to forgotten places and preserve their stories through information and photography. We hope to inspire curiosity and remind visitors that even places left behind can have extraordinary histories.
